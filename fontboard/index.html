Twelve modern sans serifs, doing the real job

Each candidate is shown in the roles the sans actually plays on the site: navigation, body copy, eyebrows, buttons and prices, paired with Cormorant Garamond headlines.

How to judge: read the 17px paragraph first, that is 90 percent of the job. Then check the prices (clear figures matter), the small caps eyebrow, and whether the button feels premium or techy. Mulish, the current choice, is first as the control.

# Competitive research: Surrey, Hampshire, Sussex garden design market
Research date: 31 Aug 2026. Method: live web research (search + direct page fetches). Every claim verified on the cited page. "Not published" means checked and absent.

## SURREY

### 1. Graduate Landscapes (graduatelandscapes.co.uk)
Base Hampshire (Haslemere area), serves Surrey/Hampshire/London/West Sussex. Ranks #1 for "garden designer Surrey". Full-service design-to-build for affluent country/estate homeowners. 3 stages: Design Consultation, Garden Design, Project Management + Landscaping. Pricing not published. CTA: arrange a design consultation. Trust: RHS Gold, BALI, ISO, CHAS. Addresses implementation risk (in-house team, dedicated PM). Maintenance service = recurring revenue. Location pages name Virginia Water, St George's Hill, Cobham, Farnham, Guildford, Weybridge. Weaknesses: no pricing, PM only for own builds, vague stages.

### 2. Wild by Design (wildby.design)
Dorking. Sustainability-led design-and-build, attacks the design-then-handoff model ("single point of contact"). 3 phases with fixed price after phase 1; concepts within a week. PUBLISHED cost guide: most projects £20k-£80k; small £5k-£15k, medium £15k-£40k, large £40k-£100k+; designer time £50-£100/hr (wildby.design/landscaping-costs/). Trust: BALI, Surrey Wildlife Trust partner, Pro Landscaper award, 23 five-star Google reviews, 100+ homeowners since 2018. Strongest local SEO: town pages (Guildford, Woking, Weybridge), Sussex page, cost guide, blog. Weaknesses: young firm, eco-brand may not fit formal tastes, no independent PM.

### 3. Simon Orchard Gardens (simonorchardgardens.com)
Petersfield, pages for Surrey/Hampshire/West Sussex/London. Solo premium designer, "no templates". 6 steps ending in build by recommended contractors + optional monitoring. Fee guidance buried in 2019 blog: ~£5k fees on ~£50k build; design fee 15-20% small projects, 10% or lower large. CTA: "Tell us about your garden", photos + personal reply within 48 hours (best lead offer seen). Trust: Houzz Best in Design 9 years, BBC One "Your Garden Made Perfect", 21 five-star Google reviews. Weaknesses: one-man capacity, cost guidance buried, handoff gap on build, no maintenance income, no SGD/BALI/APL.

### 4. We Love Plants / Nic Howard (we-love-plants.co.uk)
Outwood, Surrey + Sussex. Horticulture-first premium design with aftercare. Pillars: Design / Planting / Aftercare. Pricing not published. CTA: Book a Consultation. Trust: SGD, Chelsea + Hampton Court awards, Sussex Heritage Trust, House & Garden, Gardens Illustrated press. Best FAQ in Surrey set: design 3-6 months, build 4-16 weeks, plant establishment 1-3 years. Strong aftercare story (seasonal refreshes). Weaknesses: no pricing, no PM narrative, weak town SEO.

### 5. Raine Garden Design (rainegardendesign.co.uk)
Farnham. Award-led boutique ("sanctuaries of tranquility"). 5 stages incl. optional overseeing via APL landscapers; staged payments. Pricing not published. Trust: APL Designer of the Year 2024, APL Gold 2024/2021, 5 RHS Hampton Court medals, HTA, Trustmark. Weaknesses: design-only, no maintenance, no pricing, thin objection handling, no location pages.

### 6. Bluefish Landscaping (bluefishlandscapes.co.uk)
Farnham. Contractor-led concept-to-completion since 2007; also builds for other designers. Design & Planning (complimentary pre-project advice) / Construction / Maintain. Pricing not published but promises fully quantified quotes. Trust: BALI Award Winner 2024, supplier partnerships, designer testimonials. Addresses mess, communication (fortnightly meetings), quote transparency. Estate maintenance = recurring. Weaknesses: contractor DNA, less aspirational brand.

### 7. Rae Wilkinson (raewilkinson.com)
Knepp Estate, West Sussex; markets Surrey first. Sustainability/wellbeing luxury designer. Monitors "throughout the entire process". Pricing not published. Trust: RHS Gold Chatsworth, Silver-Gilt Hampton Court, Pro Landscaper Best Planting 2022, ~30 years. Weaknesses: no build arm, no maintenance, no process/fee transparency, no location pages.

### 8. Matthew Childs Design (matthewchildsdesign.co.uk)
Reigate. Top-tier studio. 3 named stages: Creating a Vision / Detailing and Specification / Implementation (site monitoring, JCLI contract administration, budget tracking). Pricing not published. Trust: RHS Chelsea 2024, House & Garden Top 50 3 years, SGD Awards. Weaknesses: national positioning, weak town SEO, formal tone, no aftercare revenue.

### 9. Claire Merriman Design (clairemerrimandesign.co.uk)
Shamley Green, Guildford. Approachable full-service; closest to homeowner PM pitch ("my team can manage your project from start to finish"). PUBLISHED: initial consultation £495-£895 +VAT (£50 to NSPCC); projects £2,000-£600,000. Weaknesses: thin site, near-zero trust signals; holds Blossom's positioning in keywords but executes weakly. Title targets "Landscape Design and Garden Project Management Surrey - Guildford".

## HAMPSHIRE

### 10. Ann-Marie Powell Gardens (ann-mariepowell.com)
Petersfield. Top-of-market studio brand (Spear's Index). PUBLISHED small-project pricing: online consult 60min £800; in-person 90min £1,500; Small Project Design £3,000 inc VAT (build up to £20k / 100 sq m); £5,500 inc VAT (£20k-£50k / 100-250 sq m). Trust: SGD Fellow, SGD Awards 2021/22, RHS Gold x2 + Silver-Gilt, BBC People's Choice, House & Garden Top 50 five years. Weaknesses: celebrity entry pricing, no build arm, no PM product, no aftercare, no local landing pages.

### 11. Helyers of Hampshire (helyers.co.uk)
Fareham area, county-wide. Volume design-and-build. 2D/3D design then install with on-site PM. Free consultation + free site visit; WhatsApp booking. Trust: RHS Silver-Gilt, Trustpilot. Strongest Hampshire local SEO: town pages for a dozen+ towns (Winchester, Basingstoke, Romsey, New Forest...). Weaknesses: trade-brand feel (driveways, artificial grass), no designer credentials, no pricing.

### 12. Stuart Charles Towner (stuartcharlestowner.co.uk)
Hampshire. Design-led premium studio. Named stages: Initial Briefing / Survey / Concept Design / Developed Design. Pricing not published. Trust: SGD Registered Member, APL, RHS Gold + Silver-Gilt medals (Chelsea, Hampton Court), Best of Houzz 2023. Weaknesses: no blog, no location pages, no packaged entry offer, digitally passive.

### 13. Adam Vetere (adamvetere.co.uk)
Winchester. Owner-designer, builds via external contractors with Adam project managing (client quote: "Adam project managed the whole process brilliantly"). Budget anchor published: "overall budgets typically ranging from £20,000 to £250,000". Trust: Best of Houzz 2025, 11 named testimonials. Winchester + Guildford town pages. Weaknesses: thin maintenance offer, one-man capacity, no professional-body membership shown.

## SUSSEX

### 14. Acres Wild (acreswild.co.uk)
Nuthurst, near Horsham. Established 1988, top-end country gardens. 7-stage SGD-style process incl. "project monitoring". Pricing not published. Trust: SGD Registered Practice, multiple SGD Awards incl. Grand Award 2014, House & Garden Top 50 2021-2025, Country Life Top 100. Weaknesses: static portfolio site, monitoring not full PM, nothing for smaller budgets, no recurring revenue.

### 15. Your Garden Design / David Loy (yourgardendesign.co.uk)
Chichester. Award-led bespoke. Cleanest packaging in Sussex: Design / Softscape / Build / Maintain ("Tailored Garden & Landscape Curation"). Pricing not published. Trust: MSGD, BSc Hort, SGD Award 2015. Weaknesses: no CTAs, no testimonials shown, no local pages; beatable on search.

### 16. Nigel L Philips (nigelphilips.co.uk)
Lewes. Since 1981. Bespoke design + productised "One-to-One" design-in-a-day. PUBLISHED: Option One 5hrs £450 (up to 400 sq m); Option Two 6hrs £750; Option Three 7hrs £1,500 (over 400 sq m). Trust: SGD Fellow, BALI, APL. Weaknesses: dated site, no build/PM, no maintenance. His design-day is the entry-level pricing benchmark.

### 17. Alex Bell Garden Design (alexbellgardendesign.co.uk)
Steyning. "Plant-rich" bespoke gardens. Pricing not published. Trust claims (Hampton Court, Malvern awards, 30 years) without proof points. Weaknesses: thin trust architecture, no process, no packages, no recurring services.

## Cross-market patterns

- Process convention: 3-7 stages, always consultation > survey > concept/masterplan > detailed drawings + planting plan > build (theirs or contractor's) > optional aftercare. Design and build structurally separated at the premium end.
- Fee convention: "fee proposal after consultation" is the default. Published anywhere: only Ann-Marie Powell, Nigel Philips, Claire Merriman (consult fee), Wild by Design (cost guide), Simon Orchard (2019 blog), Adam Vetere (budget anchor).
- Trust convention: RHS medals + SGD/BALI/APL badges are table stakes at the top; Google/Houzz review counts cited by design-build firms.

## What NOBODY does (Blossom's openings)
1. Independent client-side project management for homeowners as a standalone product (any designer/contractor combination). Closest: Openview (Kent, dated), Upcher & Co (Southampton, dated).
2. Published, packaged pricing at full-project level.
3. Productised year-round care programme (named annual programme, seasonal calendar).
4. Serious objection-handling copy / FAQ (only We Love Plants and Bluefish try).
5. Premium positioning + town-level SEO discipline together.

# Blossom Garden Design & Project Management: strategy
Drafted 31 Aug 2026 from the competitive research in /research. All pricing marked [RECOMMENDATION] is a commercial recommendation based on competitor evidence, not a market fact. Final numbers need Damo's sign-off.

## 1. Recommended market position

**The accountable lead.** Every competitor is either a designer who hands you to a builder, or a builder who designs. Nobody in Surrey, Hampshire or Sussex stands beside the client and runs the whole project on their behalf, whoever designs and whoever builds. Blossom takes that seat.

Position statement (internal): Blossom is the design and delivery practice for homeowners who want a beautiful garden without running a construction project. We design gardens that can actually be built, and we manage the build so they actually get built well.

Why this wins (evidence):
- Independent client-side PM is whitespace in all three counties (research: only Openview/Kent and Upcher/Southampton adjacent, both dated).
- The market's own convention (SGD Stage F) treats build oversight as an hourly afterthought.
- Homeowner pain is documented: defects they cannot assess, underquoting then corner-cutting, abandonment, no one holding payment milestones.
- Transparency is a proven differentiator used by almost nobody (2 of 18 publish pricing).

## 2. Proposition

What does Blossom do? Designs gardens and manages garden projects across Surrey, Hampshire and Sussex, as one service or separately.

Who is it for? Homeowners who care about design and want the project handled properly: time-poor professionals, families post-renovation, new movers, owners of established gardens.

What problem does it remove? The gap between a drawing and a delivered garden: coordinating designer, landscaper and suppliers, judging quality, controlling budget, chasing progress.

Why Blossom rather than DIY coordination? One accountable lead. Design grounded in budget and site from day one. Independent oversight of quality and payment milestones. Fewer expensive mistakes, misunderstandings and delays.

Why is independent PM valuable? The person checking the work is not the person selling it. Payments released against completed, inspected work. A professional on the client's side of the table.

Why now? Gardens take 6 to 12 months from first meeting to finished space. Start in autumn or winter, and the garden is ready for its first summer. Contractor diaries and plant lead times reward early planning. (Evidence-backed narrative, used honestly.)

**Core promise: A beautiful garden, properly delivered.**

## 3. Service architecture (5 categories)

1. **Garden Consultation** (entry point). 90 minutes at the property + a written summary: what the garden could be, realistic budget bands, recommended route. [RECOMMENDATION] £295, fully redeemed against any design or management fee. Evidence: premium convention is paid + redeemable (£195 London Garden Designer; regional range £195-£895; free = volume signal).
2. **Garden Design** (design only). Concept and masterplan, detailed construction drawings, planting design; also design reviews of existing plans and renovation plans for improving what's there. Client can take drawings to any landscaper, with or without our management. [RECOMMENDATION] publish "design fees typically 10 to 15 percent of the build budget, agreed as a fixed fee before work starts; most full designs start around £3,500". Evidence: SGD scale + London Garden Designer from £3,250 + regional 10-15% norms.
3. **Independent Project Management** (the differentiator). For clients with a design (ours or anyone's): budget and feasibility check, tender preparation, contractor selection, contract and payment milestones, weekly site inspections, quality control, snagging and handover. Works with the client's own designer or landscaper. [RECOMMENDATION] fixed monthly fee from £950/month during the build, or 10 percent of build cost for full tender-to-handover management; state clearly on site as "as a guide". Evidence: whitespace (no published benchmark exists; anchored to SGD Stage F scope and design-fee norms).
4. **Complete Garden Transformation** (end-to-end flagship). Consultation through design, tender, build management, planting and handover. One lead, one point of accountability. [RECOMMENDATION] guidance copy: "most complete projects sit between £30,000 and £80,000 all-in; we design to an agreed budget from day one" (evidence: Wild by Design £20k-£80k; Rebecca Read/Wild Butterfly from £20k; Vetere £20k-£250k anchor).
5. **The Garden Year** (year-round follow-on). A named annual care programme: four seasonal visits (planting review, seasonal adjustments, forward plan), a written garden calendar, oversight of maintenance contractors if wanted. [RECOMMENDATION] from £180 per seasonal visit or an annual programme from £650. Evidence: whitespace locally; Wild by Design proves premium recurring pricing works in Surrey.

Clear distinctions the site must draw: design only / management only / complete service / consultation entry / year-round care.

Pricing publication verdict: PUBLISH indicative "from" pricing and fee models. Rationale: transparency is near-absent locally, it pre-qualifies affluent clients, it feeds the cost-guide SEO opportunity, and evidence supports every number band we publish. All prices marked "as a guide" on site.

## 4. Customer segments and buying situations

1. Overwhelmed dreamers: know they want change, don't know where to start. Entry: consultation.
2. Design-ready, delivery-anxious: have a plan or designer, scared of the build. Entry: independent PM. (Unserved segment.)
3. Time-poor professionals and families: want it handled. Entry: complete transformation.
4. New movers: house right, garden wrong. Entry: consultation or complete.
5. Post-renovation households: builders gone, garden wrecked. Entry: design or complete.
6. Established garden owners: want renewal and stewardship. Entry: Garden Year or planting design.

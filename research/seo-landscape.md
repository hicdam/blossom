# SEO and search-demand landscape: Surrey, Hampshire, Sussex
Research date: 31 Aug 2026. Competition levels are estimates from observable SERP density (no volume data used). Sources verified by fetch.

## Keyword tiers

### Tier 1: core commercial (high intent)
- "garden designer surrey": HIGH. 8+ specialists with keyword in title (Graduate, Raine, Simon Orchard, Hugh Tanton, We Love Plants, Rae Wilkinson).
- "garden designer hampshire/winchester": HIGH-ish. Houzz Winchester directory ranks (135 pros); Vetere, Charlies Gardens, Robert Kennett, Taylor Tripp, Loam.
- "garden designer sussex": MEDIUM-HIGH, mixed quality. Facebook page on page 1 signals softer SERP.

### Tier 2: service keywords
- "garden design and build [surrey]": VERY HIGH (whole firm category).
- "landscape design [county]": MEDIUM-HIGH (Taylor Tripp, Neil Jones, Roeder).
- "planting design [town]": LOW-MEDIUM. Opportunity (only Greenarden Esher/Weybridge dedicated page found).
- "garden makeover/renovation": MEDIUM, dominated by national cost guides. No local owner.
- "garden project management": LOW locally. Only in-house PM pages (Graduate, Aralia).

### Tier 3: towns (dedicated-page proxy for demand)
- Guildford: heaviest (6+ pages + exact-match domain). Hard to crack.
- Weybridge/Woking: medium-high (Wild by Design pages, Houzz 773 pros).
- Cobham/Esher: medium demand, WEAK dedicated coverage relative to affluence. OPPORTUNITY.
- Farnham/Godalming/Haslemere: dated template competition. OPPORTUNITY.
- Winchester: medium-high (2 strong town pages).
- Alresford: 1 dedicated page (Gadsden). LOW competition, affluent. OPPORTUNITY.
- Romsey: no dedicated competitor page. LOW. OPPORTUNITY.
- Petersfield/Midhurst: low page competition, but Ann-Marie Powell brand heavyweight present.
- Haywards Heath: LOW-MEDIUM. OPPORTUNITY.
- Chichester/Horsham: medium (directories + Sussex Landscapers).

### Tier 4: long-tail homeowner questions (verified in live FAQs/guides)
Cost: "How much will my garden cost to design?", "How much should I spend?", "Can I renovate on a budget?" (Aralia 41-question FAQ, Linsey Evans, MyJobQuote, Checkatrade, Ideal Home). National platforms own generic; NO local firm answers "cost in Surrey/Hampshire".
Process: "How do I commission a garden designer?", "How long from consultation to completion?", "What are the stages?", "Can a garden be built in stages?", "Can we use our own contractors?", "Do you provide a project management service?" (Linsey Evans, verbatim).
Vs: "Do I need a landscaper AND a garden designer?" (Hollandscapes, Gardens Illustrated, Bowles & Wyer). Every answer ends "hire a design-build firm". Blossom's third-option answer is differentiated.
Timing: "When is the best time to start your garden design?" (Aralia, housedesigner, Redcliffe, Francis Rose). LOW-MEDIUM.
Small gardens: "best design for small garden", size minimums. MEDIUM.

## PM gap check
Genuine whitespace: job boards partially absorb the query; design-build firms sell PM only inside their package (Graduate, Aralia, Cameron, 4thD, Living Colour). Closest real competitors: Openview (Kent, "project manage schemes being built by other contractors throughout Kent, Sussex and Surrey", dated site) and Upcher & Co (Southampton, independent consultancy, dated site). Valley Projects leads with "complete residential landscape project management" across all three counties. Caveat: exact-phrase volume likely low; win via the surrounding question space and conversion quality.

## Local SEO tactics of page-1 winners
1. Dedicated town/county location pages (Wild by Design 80+ villages; Vetere; Simon Orchard county pages; Gadsden Alresford).
2. "Award-Winning" + trade-body badges in title tags and on page (Raine, Simon Orchard, Wild by Design; SGD/BALI/APL directories behind them).
3. Houzz presence: directory pages rank page 1 for 6+ target towns; a reviewed profile = second listing.
4. On-page reviews + hyper-local specificity (landmarks, named villages, town-named portfolios).
5. Blogs/editorial ranking for town terms; winter-planning content genre proven (Vivid Gardens, housedesigner).
6. Citations: Houzz, Book a Builder, Rated People, Get Surrey directory, Gardenvisit.

## Seasonality
- Spring surge (RHS Chelsea/Malvern/Gardeners' World in May); Dorset studio +30% Jan-Jul after Chelsea showcase (Yahoo News).
- Winter Nov-Jan is the published planning season (Vivid Gardens; housedesigner: 6-12 months lead time).
- Content calendar: demand-capture Mar-May; demand-creation Oct-Jan.

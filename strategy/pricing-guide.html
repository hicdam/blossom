How the fee is built

Agree the working build budget first. Pick the percentage from the complexity you saw. Multiply. That is the fixed fee, and it does not move if the build price moves later. The floor is £3,500.

How to turn a budget into a quote

All-in budget divided by 1.2 is the build. The rest is Blossom. So on the standard package a £60,000 budget means about £50,000 of build and £10,000 for Blossom.

    The rules that never change

    As a guide, until it is in writingEvery number on the website and in this guide is a guide. The proposal is the price.
    No price without a visitThe Garden Review is the only thing priced before you have stood in the garden.
    Nothing hiddenAny supplier commission, procurement fee or product margin is written into the proposal before the client says yes.
    Third-party costs at costSurveys, engineers, planning fees and supplier invoices pass through at cost. Blossom's fee is Blossom's fee.
    Travel your callWithin 20 miles of Arford is included. 20 to 40 miles adds £45 a visit. Over 40 miles adds £95 a visit, or say no.
    Urgency your callWork needed to start inside two weeks adds 25% to Blossom's fee. It costs you other clients' time.
    Day rate for extras your call£600 a day, £80 an hour. Used for extra revision rounds, extra visits and anything outside the appointment.
    VAT your callDecide whether Blossom registers. Below the £90,000 turnover threshold you do not have to. Until then, prices are the price.

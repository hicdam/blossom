# Pricing, fees and independent PM: verified market evidence
Research date: 31 Aug 2026. All figures read on live pages. Full source URLs preserved here for citation.

## A. How UK garden designers charge

### SGD official fee guidance (primary source)
"Engaging a Garden Designer: Guidance for Clients on Professional Fees" (s3.amazonaws.com/LibraryFiles/SGD%20Fee%20Guidance.pdf, Rev. 2025):
- Three fee bases: time-charge, lump sum, or percentage of landscaping contract cost.
- Percentage scale: ~21-22% at £30k contract cost sliding to ~8% at £500k. Worked examples: £30k build = 21% full design fee; £100k build = 10%.
- Stage split: Outline Proposals 25% / Design Detail 40% / Tender Process 10% / Planting Detail 25%.
- Initial consultation (Stage A) and site supervision (Stage F: contract administration, site inspections, certification of payments, snagging) are "other services" charged hourly. Standard contract: JCLI Consultancy Agreement for Home Owner/Occupier.

### Published designer figures
- London Garden Designer (SGD, London): consultation £195+VAT refunded if proceeding; design fees 10-15% of build budget; full design from ~£3,250+VAT. (londongardendesigner.com/faqs)
- Kristina Clode (Rye, Sussex, SGD): build £250-£450/sq m; design fees 10-15% of build; topo survey ~£650; plants from £65/sq m; consultation £1,200 inc VAT. (kristinaclodegardendesign.co.uk)
- DP Garden Design (Alresford, Hampshire): % of build; example 10% of £80k = £8,000; SGD-style stage split; surveys £450-£900; PM during construction billed hourly. (dpgardendesign.co.uk/what-does-a-garden-design-cost/)
- Ernest & Edward (Durham): ~18% at £10k sliding to 12% at £60k; free consult within 30 miles.
- Tythorne (Lincs): fixed concept plans £725/£925/£1,125 by garden size; planting plans £475-£675; lighting plans £275-£475.
- Rebecca Read (Devon): site visit from £240; concept £960-£14,850 by size band; redesign package from £3,785; build budgets "typically start from £20,000".
- MyJobQuote (Jul 2026): designers £60-£200/hr; fixed design fees £600-£4,500; average £30-£150 per sq m.
- Houzz UK guide: design fees 8-20% of project budget; small garden design fees £2,000-£4,500.
- housedesigner.com: £60-£200/hr, average ~£125; ~10% design only, up to 20% design and build.

### Consultation conventions
Paid and redeemable is the premium convention (£195 London Garden Designer, £50 Cedar deducted); free is the volume convention (Tythorne, Ernest & Edward, Helyers). Checkatrade snippet (unverified, 403): on-site consultations £150-£350. Regional premium range observed: £195 to £895 (Claire Merriman £495-£895) to £800/£1,500 (Ann-Marie Powell).

### Build cost guidance published
- £250-£450/sq m premium (Kristina Clode); £100-£150/sq m basic hard landscaping (Garden Ninja); £300-£350/sq m intensive (Cotswold E&G); small design-build "at least £20,000", larger "at least £30,000" (Wild Butterfly); most Surrey projects £20k-£80k (Wild by Design).
- "10-20% of house value" appears only in landscaper blogs (North Hill Gardens), not authority sources. Treat as soft meme, not a rule.

## B. Independent garden PM for homeowners

- The Landscaping Expert (UK-wide): the only clear independent client-side consultancy found; time-based pricing, no figures. (landscapingexpert.co.uk)
- Aralia, The Landscape Consultancy, Cotswold E&G: PM bundled inside turnkey/design-build only.
- Designer convention: "project monitoring" / "inspection" as optional hourly add-on (SGD Stage F, London Garden Designer, Cedar, DP Garden Design). Nobody productises residential PM with fixed fee or %.
- Openview Landscape Design (Kent): manages schemes built by other contractors across Kent, Sussex, Surrey; dated site. Upcher & Co (Southampton): independent design + PM consultancy for Hampshire; dated site, no town pages.
- Homeowner pain evidence: Mumsnet "Garden renovation gone wrong" (bodged wall, wrong levels, bad grout, suspected underquoting); Dudley Trading Standards prosecution (deposits taken, work stalled, no-show labourers); Telford case (property damage, threats for extra £1,000). Failure modes: quality defects the homeowner cannot assess, underquoting then corner-cutting, abandonment, no independent party holding payment milestones.
- Demand-side: Linsey Evans FAQ includes "Do you provide a project management service?" and "Can we use our own contractors?"

## C. Year-round revenue services

- Wild by Design maintenance: named team, fixed annual price, detailed schedule; fortnightly/monthly visits Mar-Oct, lighter winter; £45-£65 per gardener-hour vs typical Surrey £25-£35.
- Tythorne: planting plans and lighting plans as fixed-price products.
- Planting Partners: garden coaching from £40/session, seasonal programmes.
- Garden Ninja: online consultation £250/hr; day rate £650.
- Wild Butterfly: weekly/monthly/quarterly maintenance programmes; border renovation from £1,100 inc VAT.
- Seasonality: enquiries peak at the start of the year and spring (Heard Landscapes; Wildings Studio notes the May show-season surge). Trade actively promotes autumn/winter as best time to design and build (Sarah Link Surrey, Vivid Gardens Nov-Jan window, housedesigner.com; 6-12 months first meeting to finished garden).

## Implications (safe-to-adopt vs whitespace)
1. SAFE: design fee ~10-15% of build sliding scale (up to ~20% small, ~8-10% at £100k+). Anchored by SGD Rev. 2025 + regional designers.
2. SAFE: SGD stage split (25/40/10/25) and staged invoicing.
3. SAFE: paid initial consultation ~£195-£400, redeemable against design fees.
4. SAFE to quote as guidance: build £100-£150/sq m basic to £250-£450/sq m premium; realistic projects from ~£20,000.
5. WHITESPACE: independent client-side PM, productised (fixed monthly fee or % with defined visit cadence, payment certification, snagging). Legible to trade via SGD Stage F language ("owner's representative").
6. WHITESPACE: named year-round care programme with seasonal calendar.
7. Marketing angle: use failure-mode CATEGORIES (not individual cases) for objection copy.
8. "Design in autumn, build in winter, enjoy in spring" is a validated demand-creation narrative.
